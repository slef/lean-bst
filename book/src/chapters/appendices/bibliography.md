# Bibliography

The works cited in this book, sorted by the names of their authors. Each entry links to the published version (DOI) and, where one exists, to a freely available preprint. The line under an entry lists the pages that cite it; each link goes to the first citation on that page. Each part ends with bibliographic notes that say who proved what, and when: the [notes on models](notes-models.html), on [bounds](notes-bounds.html), on [algorithms](notes-algorithms.html) and on [structures](notes-structures.html).
